# Experimental

***Warning: WIP, less tested, disabled by default***

Each entry can be switched on in the config, under `Experimental`.

## Hammer Upgrades

- **Dual Moonshot** (Staff) — No longer costs range and fuse to fire the extra projectile.
- **Reaper Knives → Hook Knives** (Dagger) — Reverted to its EA-launch implementation.
- **Pharaoh Etchings** (Witch's Staff) — No longer launches Lone Shades from Omega Special, instead your Punishing Fields last 50% longer.
- **Hidden Knives** (Dagger) — Fires in a Spiral pattern around you (like Spiral Knives in EA-launch), with extra knives. Firing angle overridden by Sureshot Flurry if also held.
- **Enduring Coil** (Torch) — Special projectiles last +100% longer (double) rather than adding a flat +2 Sec., and applies to the regular special projectile as well.
- **Hidden Helix** (Torch) — Gain +2 extra projectiles instead of +1.
- **Rising Helix** (Torch) — Makes bonus damage increased to 50%.
- **Whirling Helix** (Torch) — Projectile speed bonus increased to 100%.

## Weapon Aspects

- **Aspect of Anubis** (Witch's Staff) — Ankh Scepter's Dash-Strike still creates its damaging field, but its regular Attack is the normal staff combo. Attack and Omega Attack launch Lone Shades at foes struck; Special and Omega Special draw foes in. `AnkhSwapSecondaries` swaps the two.
- **Aspect of Supay** (Umbral Flames) — Rush-boon damage bonus restored to EA levels.

## Other

- **Arachne's Dresses** (`Experimental.Other.ArachneDresses`) — A dress whose Armor breaks comes back whenever you have Armor again from any other source, without Armor of its own, and comes off again while you have none.

## Other Mods

- **HermesDuos** (`Experimental.Mods.HermesDuos`, needs Wistiti-HermesDuos installed)
  - **Flawless Execution** becomes an Apollo x Hermes duo, replacing Golden Prodigy.
  - **Distress Call** becomes an Aphrodite x Hermes duo, replacing Adoration Fee.
  - **Flawless Execution** and **Distress Call** both removed as Hermes legendaries.
  - **Paid Dues** also counts Water boons, and **Double Time** also counts Air boons, for their requirement and the 8-boon guarantee.

- **MoreDashBoons** (`Experimental.Mods.MoreDashBoons`, needs zerp-More_Dash_Boons installed). Every dash boon is replaced by a copy, and like a core slot you're offered only one, or two while you hold Family Trade (MoreDuos); each one you hold fires from the same dash.
  - **Flame Blink** — counts as a fireball from Hestia (Burning Meteor, Scalding Vapor).
  - **Flutter Blink** — arrows repeat with Ripple Effect.
  - **Sworn Blink** — compatible with MoreDuos Volcanic Rift.

## Keepsakes
- **Concave Stone** (Echo) — New Effect: Chance to copy your next Boon or Hammer reward instead.
- **Evil Eye** (Nemesis) — Damage bonus now applied to the last foe that hit you.
- **Metallic Droplet** (Hermes) — Now keeps half its move/strike/cast speed boost once the timer runs out while held.
- **White Antler** (Artemis) — Keeps its effects as long as its held. Max health increased to 30.
- **Olympian Keepsakes** — New Heroic rarity, can transform boons of any rarity into Legendary (or Heroic, if Legendary is already offered / held) instead.
